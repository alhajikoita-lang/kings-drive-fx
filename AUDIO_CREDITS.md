# Audio sources

KINGS DRIVE FX currently uses the CC0 racing car engine loop pack by domasx2 from OpenGameArt.

Source page: https://opengameart.org/content/racing-car-engine-sound-loops

License: CC0

The six loop files are streamed directly from OpenGameArt and blended by simulated RPM. The procedural Web Audio engine remains underneath the samples for bass, texture and mode variation.
